# Knovaryn brand guidelines

The Knovaryn identity expresses one sentence: **every training example,
traced to its source.** The mark is that sentence, drawn once.

## The symbol — "Tracemark"

The Knovaryn K is a provenance route:

- the **stem** is the source document — the fixed spine of truth;
- the **arm** is three segmented evidence spans converging inward — chunks
  and source spans on their way to become data;
- the **gate ring** at the junction is the quality gate — nothing passes
  through by accident;
- the **leg** is the verified example — one solid, continuous stroke leaving
  the gate.

The wordmark's K is the same construction, so the symbol and the name are
never two different ideas.

## Palette

| Token | Light value | Dark value | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1C2B33` | `#ECF2EF` (as text) | structure, text, monochrome marks |
| Trace | `#0E7C6B` | `#2EB39A` | the provenance route, verification, links |
| Paper | `#FBFAF7` | `#0F1B21` | surfaces |
| Signal amber | `#B45309` | `#D97706` | quarantine/review semantics (sparing) |

Full token definitions live in the [design system](../../design-system/index.md).
The mark itself uses only ink, trace, and the background color of its gate
core — never gradients inside the symbol.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- Keep clear space of **¼ of the mark's height** on all sides of any lockup.
- Minimum sizes: symbol **16 px** raster / **20 px** vector; horizontal
  lockup **120 px** wide; wordmark alone **96 px** wide.
- Below 24 px, prefer the symbol-on-tile (`favicon.svg`) over the bare mark.

## Misuse

Do not:

- fill the gate ring solid or remove its contrasting core (it stops reading
  as a gate);
- close the arm's segment gaps or add more segments;
- recolor the mark outside the palette, apply gradients to it, or place it
  on busy imagery without a scrim;
- stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows to any lockup;
- redraw the wordmark in a font — the letterforms are original geometry and
  are part of the identity;
- use the mark inside another logo or as a generic "data" icon.

## Reproduction

Every asset in this directory is generated by
`scripts/visuals/build_brand_assets.py` from a single geometry source.
To change the identity, change the generator and rerun it — never hand-edit
the outputs (a drift check enforces this). The wordmark uses no font files:
its letterforms are original vector paths, so no font license or runtime
font dependency applies.
